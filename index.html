Materials

knife

bowl

pot/frying pan

Stainless frying strainer

Plastic pouch

Chopping board

Vegetable pillar

Stainless food clip

Round tupperware container

Strainer(for removing water)

Ingredients

kamote/sweet potato 1/2 kl

White sugar

1 liter of oil

Preparation of ingredients

Step 1: Prepare the needed equipments.

bowl of water for soaking the sweet potato

chopping board

knife

vegetable pillar or cutter if available


Step 2: peeling time!

remove the skin of the sweet potato using the vegetable pillar.

After, put it in a bowl of water to soak and wash.

After that put it on the chopper board to cut in half.

Use a vegetable pillar or cutter to cut it into tiny thin pieces.

Cooking

Step 1: Prepare the necessary tools for cooking.

pot/frying pan

oil

strainer

stainless frying strainer

stainless food clip


Step 2: Putting the sweet potato in a frying pan or pot.

wash the cut tiny thin sweet potato chips

prepare and heat the oil in medium fire only, after put the thin tiny sweet potato into the pan and mix it.


Packaging

Step 1: Prepare the necessary items for packaging.

plastic tupperware

Sugar

stainless food clip

Plastic pakaging pouch

Step 2: Packaging

Put the cooked kamote chips in the plastic tupperware and add sugar and shake it.

After, put it into the plastic pouch bag.

Finished Product


Here is the finished product of organic homemade kamote chips. You can either sell it or store in the fridge to preserve crispyness. Enjoy your snack!
